TinkHack 3.0

2 Oct - 3 Oct

“Unleash your potential in a high-energy hackathon focused on innovation, technology, and AI.”

Event Overview

TinkHack 3.0, organized by TinkerHub MEC, is Kerala’s premier student hackathon focusing on Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI. This high-energy, overnight event brings together aspiring innovators, developers, and tech enthusiasts to explore cutting-edge technologies and create impactful solutions.

Participants will collaborate in teams, engage in mentorship sessions, and tackle real-world challenges across domains such as AI, social impact, automation, and technology innovation. With workshops, guidance from industry experts, and exciting prizes, TinkHack 3.0 is the ultimate platform for students to learn, experiment, and showcase their creativity.

Key Highlights:

  • Workshops & Mentorship: Gain insights from experts and refine your skills.
  • Team Collaboration: Work in teams of 2–4 to solve challenges.
  • Exciting Prizes: Recognition and awards for innovative solutions.
  • Theme: Focused on Generative AI and innovation, encouraging creative problem-solving.

Why Attend:

  • Enhance your technical and teamwork skills.
  • Network with peers and industry professionals.
  • Experience the thrill of hacking for a cause.
  • Showcase your talent in a competitive yet collaborative environment.
